# Design System

## Overview
theUG Portfolio embodies a futuristic, high-craft developer workspace and celestial observatory. The visual tone is technical, authentic, and cinematic: deep charcoal and near-black canvases paired with vibrant neon-lime and cyan telemetry lights.

## Colors
- **Void Background (`#07080B`)**: Deep cosmic space foundation.
- **Neon-Lime Primary (`#00FF66`)**: High-energy core, active status pulses, primary accents.
- **Electric Cyan (`#00E5FF`)**: Telemetry streams, secondary identifiers, orbital vectors.
- **Surface Elevation (`#0D0E12`)**: Dark frosted charcoal panels with subtle high-contrast borders.

## Typography
- **Headings & Display**: Space Grotesk (`--font-space-grotesk`) for confident, modern technical presence.
- **Data & Telemetry**: IBM Plex Mono (`--font-mono`) for console outputs, orbital coordinates, and metric specs.
- **Body Prose**: Clean sans-serif with comfortable line height (`1.6` - `2.0`) and generous letter spacing.

## Layout
- Full-bleed interactive canvas experiences with docked, non-intrusive telemetry HUDs.
- Fluid responsive grid that gracefully adapts from multi-column desktop setups to single-column mobile corridors.

## Elevation & Depth
- Layered z-depth with soft offsets and atmospheric glowing halos.
- CRT-inspired scanline vignettes and fine radial coordinate guides.

## Shapes
- Crisp, mechanical border radii (`8px` to `12px`) for panels.
- Circular planetary spheres and pill-shaped status tags (`9999px`).

## Components
- **Central Sun**: Radiant neon-lime energy core with rotating solar flares.
- **Orbital Worlds**: Glowing celestial bodies with authentic vector emblems and kinetic slingshot physics.
- **Telemetry HUD**: Glassmorphic docked card displaying live technical breakdown and ecosystem conviction.

## Do's and Don'ts
- **Do**: Use high-contrast semantic color pairings (text $\ge 4.5:1$).
- **Do**: Maintain responsive touch targets on mobile viewports.
- **Don't**: Introduce heavy 3D frameworks (Three.js) when high-DPI 2D Canvas delivers pure 120 FPS.
- **Don't**: Crowd viewports with redundant cards or unformatted text blocks.
