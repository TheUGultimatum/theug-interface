## Capabilities and Constraints
- Sections: Home, About, Projects, Labs, On-Chain Conviction, Timeline, Contact.
- Zero fabrication rule: Never invent token balances, wallet holdings, fake transaction counts, fictitious partnerships, or artificial claims.
- Minimal On-Chain Conviction: A compact title, a single introductory sentence, the central Sun with Usmaan's meme-inspired pookie face portrait, 11 orbiting worlds, and a small contextual details area upon selection. No bloated dashboards or metrics walls.
- Classification integrity: Distinguish networks (e.g. Base is a network, not a token), ecosystems, platforms (Robinhood is not automatically a chain), and native tokens. Arc retains ecosystem identity with no invented ticker.
- Performance: High-DPI 120 FPS Canvas using native Web APIs. Avoid heavy 3D frameworks (no Three.js) without justification.
- Custom cursor: Restrained, non-interfering desktop cursor; disabled on touch devices; respects prefers-reduced-motion.

## Product Principles
1. **Authenticity over inflation**: Showcase real curiosity, real experiments, and genuine ecosystem convictions without fabricated financial claims.
2. **The Solar System is the experience**: Keep On-Chain Conviction visually rich but information-light—the celestial motion leads, without intrusive dashboards.
3. **Identity preservation**: Refine and elevate the original dark terminal/hacker personality rather than replacing it with a generic template.
4. **Craft and responsiveness**: Flawless layout and kinetic interactions across 360px mobile viewports up to wide desktop screens.
